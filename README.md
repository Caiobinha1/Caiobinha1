My passion for coding began at age 16 when I wrote my first lines of C/C++. That instantly sparked my interest in technology. After spending two years working professionally as a Software Developer—focusing on databases (SQL) and Delphi—I chose to expand my horizons by entering university to study hardware and electronic systems.

Today, my academic and research work bridges the gap between hardware and software, focusing on deploying deep learning models on constrained edge devices and creating low-power environmental monitoring sensors.
